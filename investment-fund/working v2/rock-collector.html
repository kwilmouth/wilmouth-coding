How to Play

  1. Click Mine for Rocks to dig up a card.
  2. Each card reveals a unique rock with facts and details.
  3. Your goal is to collect all 20 unique rock cards.
  4. If you find a duplicate, keep trying - they're randomly chosen!
  5. Click Reset Progress to start fresh.
