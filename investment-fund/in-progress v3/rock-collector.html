How to Play

  1. Spend Energy Points to mine rocks and uncover cards.
  2. Each card reveals a unique rock, complete with facts and details.
  3. Collect all 20 unique rock cards to complete the set.
  4. Sell duplicate cards to earn Energy Points when you run low.
  5. Select Reset Game Progress to start over at any time.
